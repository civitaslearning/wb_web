## The challenge

Coordinating student success action across 53,000+ students, 18 embedded advising and student success units, and hundreds of advisors without a centralized model or collapsing operations into a single chain of command.

## The approach

Established shared leadership tables bringing together advising administrators, student affairs leaders, and frontline staff; implemented engagement-based signals rather than demographic flags; created unified visibility across institutional systems with role-based access; coordinated outreach to eliminate duplicated efforts.

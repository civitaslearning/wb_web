## The challenge

Faculty and advisors were managing student interactions through disconnected systems (Excel trackers, QLESS queues, existing scheduling platforms) with no shared visibility across departments. Previous Civitas Learning implementation carried institutional hesitation.

## The approach

Leadership conducted individual department meetings to understand workflows rather than conduct traditional demonstrations. Cross-functional teams including advisors, faculty, department chairs, student services, registrar, financial aid, admissions, and IT provided iterative feedback throughout the rollout process.

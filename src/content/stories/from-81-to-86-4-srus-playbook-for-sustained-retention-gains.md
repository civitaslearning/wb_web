## The challenge

Moving retention at a level where every half-point gain is harder to achieve than the last; sustaining momentum after achieving initial success.

## The approach

Institution-specific analytics, paired predictor analysis for precise student group identification, targeted interventions (exploratory student odyssey program, small financial barrier removal), and embedded data use in daily workflows alongside vendor partnership with adaptive responsiveness.

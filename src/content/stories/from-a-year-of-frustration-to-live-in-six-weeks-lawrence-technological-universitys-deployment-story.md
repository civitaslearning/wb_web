## The challenge

LTU's previous student success platform implementation stalled after one year with unclear progress, excessive internal resource demands, complex workflows ("14 steps to do one thing"), and lack of confidence in the product's maintainability.

## The approach

LTU selected Civitas Learning based on criteria including responsive vendor support, configuration-ready software, maintainable systems, and clear documentation. Implementation featured structured meeting protocols and rapid response cycles to technical questions.

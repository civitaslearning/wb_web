## The challenge

Delivering timely, personalized support to a diverse student population of 70,000+ across multiple campuses while identifying at-risk students, building advisor relationships, and maintaining complete student information across siloed systems.

## The approach

Transition to data-informed case management model using Civitas Learning platform, with streamlined caseload management, faculty collaboration, and scaled targeted outreach based on predictive analytics.

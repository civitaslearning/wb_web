## The challenge

The existing solution was described as "the most expensive note-taking platform" that lacked real-time insights and integrations needed for proactive student support and retention improvement.

## The approach

A cross-functional committee built a rigorous evaluation process with specific criteria: proven integrations (Canvas, Anthology Engagement, Maxient, Handshake), daily real-time data updates, institution-specific data ownership, accessibility compliance, and clear cost value. UT Tyler evaluated five to six vendors before selecting finalists.

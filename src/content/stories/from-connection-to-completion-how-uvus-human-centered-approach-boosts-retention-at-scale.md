## The challenge

Students felt disconnected from support systems, with fragmented outreach and procedural barriers limiting timely, personalized assistance, particularly for those approaching graduation or facing unexpected obstacles.

## The approach

Launch Student Success Specialists as near-peer advisors; integrate Civitas Learning's Student Impact Platform to provide predictive signals, enable early outreach, remove registration holds, and coordinate rapid responses to faculty-raised concerns through Canvas integration.

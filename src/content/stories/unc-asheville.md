## The challenge

Disconnected systems across multiple platforms prevented visibility into student needs, limiting the ability to provide timely interventions before problems escalated.

## The approach

Integrating all student data into one unified platform with real-time insights, enabling targeted outreach, daily advisor monitoring of risk indicators, and streamlined communication across support teams.

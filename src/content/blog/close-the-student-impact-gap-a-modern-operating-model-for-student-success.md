“Why do we have more data than ever — but less clarity?”

“Why do the same risk patterns reappear every term?”

“Why is it so hard to prove which interventions actually work?”

These patterns aren’t unique to your campus.

Nationally, just over [six in ten students complete a bachelor’s degree within six years](https://www.bestcolleges.com/research/college-graduation-rates/) of starting college. At community colleges, roughly [one in three students finish a credential within three years](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/ctr/undergrad-retention-graduation) when measured at 150% of the normal time.

Yet institutions are investing more than ever in retention strategy, predictive analytics, and student support technology — and investment alone doesn’t guarantee improvement. When data, workflows, and teams operate in isolation, insight fails to translate into coordinated action. That’s the **Student Impact Gap.**

What This Model Addresses

###### Five Questions at the Center of Student Success Strategy

QWhy do student success initiatives stall even when institutions invest heavily in them?

Most strategies focus on adding more — more outreach, more dashboards, more analytics. But fragmentation isn’t solved by layering on more activity. When data, workflows, and teams operate in isolation, insight fails to translate into coordinated action. That gap between insight and action is where initiatives stall.

QWhat is the Student Impact Gap?

The Student Impact Gap is the distance between what institutions know about their students and what they’re actually able to do with that knowledge. It appears when data lives in separate systems, risk is flagged but next steps aren’t clear, and interventions are launched without measuring outcomes.

QHow do colleges and universities close the Student Impact Gap?

Through a coordinated operating model — CLOSE — that connects data and teams, surfaces institution-specific patterns, operationalizes insight inside daily workflows, strengthens human judgment with transparent AI, and evaluates impact continuously so improvement compounds instead of resets.

QWhat causes advisor alert fatigue in student success platforms?

Alert fatigue happens when institutions fail to operationalize insight — risk signals are surfaced without clear next steps, outreach is generic rather than aligned to real risk, and advisors are flooded with flags they can’t act on in the time they have. The fix isn’t fewer alerts; it’s embedding action directly into existing workflows.

QHow do institutions measure the ROI of student success technology?

ROI becomes measurable when institutions close the evaluation loop — tracking which interventions work, for whom, and under what conditions. Without systematic evaluation, even strong initiatives produce outcomes that are hard to quantify and harder to defend in budget conversations.

## **What Is the Student Impact Gap?**

The Student Impact Gap appears when insight and effort fail to produce timely, coordinated action. It shows up when:

- Data lives in separate systems (SIS, LMS, CRM, financial aid, engagement tools)
- Advising teams operate without shared context
- Risk is flagged, but next steps aren’t clear
- Interventions are launched, but outcomes aren’t measured
- AI tools amplify noise instead of reinforcing judgment

It’s like a group project without a shared workspace. Everyone contributes, but no one sees the full picture. Unfortunately, student success often works the same way.

## **Why Traditional Student Success Strategies Stall**

Most student success strategies focus on adding more — more outreach, more dashboards, more analytics, more initiatives. But fragmentation isn’t solved by layering on more activity.

If your student information system doesn’t connect to advising workflows, if predictive models aren’t calibrated to your campus context, or if intervention results aren’t evaluated across student subgroups, improvement remains episodic and outcomes flatten. Without systematic coordination, even strong initiatives struggle to scale. It feels productive — until the next term resets everything. What’s missing isn’t effort. It’s design.

## **How to CLOSE the Student Impact Gap**

Across hundreds of our institutional partnerships, one lesson repeats: student success improves when systems are designed to work together — not layered on top of one another. CLOSE stands for:

C

Connect data and teams around shared context

Break down silos between your SIS, LMS, CRM, financial aid, and advising tools so every team works from the same, real-time student view.

L

Learn from institution-specific patterns, not generic benchmarks

Identify curriculum bottlenecks, policy friction like registration holds, gateway course risk, and subgroup persistence trends that actually drive outcomes on your campus.

O

Operationalize insight inside daily workflows

Move from generic outreach to coordinated, risk-aligned intervention embedded directly where advisors, faculty, and student services already work.

S

Strengthen human judgment with transparent AI

Use predictive analytics and decision support tools that enhance — not replace — advisor expertise, surfacing the right signals at the right time.

E

Evaluate impact and close the loop

Measure what works, for whom, and why. Refine strategy continuously so progress builds over time.

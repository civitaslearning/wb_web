We’re proud to share that our Chief Customer Officer, **Meredith Baker**, has been recognized as one of *The AI Show @ ASU+GSV’s* **Leading Women in AI** for 2025!

This honor celebrates more than 200 women advancing the field of artificial intelligence across EdTech, research, and real-world practice. Meredith’s recognition highlights her outstanding leadership, deep commitment to innovation, and tireless advocacy for leveraging AI to better serve customers and transform the student outcomes that matter most.

Civitas Learning is at the forefront of both predictive and generative AI technology, making it easier to quickly, accurately, and reliably get actionable answers to your questions. We are proud of Meredith’s work in shaping the future of AI to help colleges and universities improve the student outcomes that matter most.

The campaign honors recipients across three categories:

- **March 7** – EdTech Honorees
- **March 8** – Practitioner Honorees
- **March 10** – Research Honorees

We’re incredibly proud to see Meredith among this distinguished group of leaders who are shaping the future of AI. Join us in celebrating her achievement and the continued impact of women driving progress in AI.

You can view and share Meredith’s recognition [here](https://www.asugsvsummit.com/show/women-ai-in-edu?72a9cb16_page=2).

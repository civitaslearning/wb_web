Civitas Learning reads data from the systems you already run. That data stays yours. This page collects the documents that say how.

## Student records and FERPA

Civitas Learning works as a **school official** that your institution designates under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). The terms are set out in the [data sharing agreement](/termsandconditions-archive/dsa/), which also covers the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulations for institutions that need them. Our [privacy policy](/privacy/) states how we treat personal information as FERPA-compliant stewards of institutional data.

## Access

Users sign in with your institution's single sign-on. A username and password can be configured in place of single sign-on at your request. Access rules for each product are listed in the [platform specifications](/termsandconditions/platform-specifications/).

## What we collect and how it flows

The [standard data specifications](/termsandconditions/standard-data-specifications/) list the records the platform reads from your student information system, learning management system, and other sources, and how they are used.

## Accessibility

We are committed to WCAG 2.0 Level AA conformance and can provide Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates on request. See the [accessibility statement](/accessibility/).
